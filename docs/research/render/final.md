# Render track research: Three.js 0.186 split-screen renderer and camera rig

Scope: `src/render/*`, `src/scene/camera.ts`, `src/scene/layout.ts`, `src/scene/hud.ts` (plus `src/scene/minimap.ts`). Everything below was checked against the installed packages in `node_modules` (three 0.186.1, @types/three 0.186.0, typescript 5.9, vitest 5) and against the sources listed in section 6. All TypeScript in section 3 typechecks under the repo's `tsconfig.json` options plus `noUnusedLocals`/`noUnusedParameters`, and the pure parts (layout, camera, map-to-blocks, hull geometry) pass 19 vitest tests. The verification log is in the appendix.

## 1. The problem, in the words of the spec

- "The target is convincing Venice at projector distance, at 60 fps with 4 viewports. Realism comes from light, water and materials far more than from polygon count." (section 9)
- "3D: Three.js, plain (no React in the game loop) ... split screen via scissor viewports" (section 11); "render(state, events, layout) // one camera per team, scissor viewports" (interfaces between tracks); "Timestep: fixed 60 Hz simulation; the renderer interpolates between steps." (section 8)
- Layouts on a 16:9 screen, fixed for the round: 2 teams side by side; 3 teams 2x2 with the spare cell as a live overview map; 4 teams 2x2 with the minimap where the cells meet; 5 teams 3x2 plus overview; 6 teams 3x2 with the minimap at the crossing. "Each viewport has a thick border in the team colour and the team name." (section 2)
- Camera: "Sits about 9 m behind and 4 m above the boat, along the aim direction. It looks level, so the horizon runs through the centre of the view and the boat sits in the lower part of the frame." "A small arrow at the bottom of the viewport shows the bow against the aim direction." Kill-cam: "its cell shows a 5 s kill-cam on the boat that sank it, with a countdown, then cuts back to the respawned boat." (sections 2, 4, 5)
- Look: golden hour, "A warm HDRI sky (Poly Haven, free CC0), a low sun, soft haze in the distance." Water: "dark green-teal with animated normal maps, sky reflection and foam behind moving boats. No mirror-style planar reflections: they double the render cost of every viewport." Buildings generated from the grid with plaster, Istrian stone, algae band, shutters, chimneys. Gondola "long, black, slightly asymmetric hull with the iron bow piece (ferro), trim in the team colour, and one tall lantern at the bow glowing in the team colour". Crew: Mixamo GLB, "If importing eats too much time, use simple stylised characters generated in code ... Decide this in the first hour, not later." (section 9)
- Performance rules: "Share geometry and materials; use instancing for windows, poles and lanterns; merge building meshes per block. Render the shadow map once per frame, not once per viewport. Let the haze hide the far edge; simplify buildings beyond about 120 m. Measure with 4 viewports by the second checkpoint." Cut order: "Cut draw distance, shadows, then building detail." (sections 9, 14)
- The map: "one ASCII grid in shared/map.ts, 8 m per cell, about 50 x 38 cells" (400 x 300 m); "Coordinates: metres, Y up, water surface at y = 0, map origin at the centre. The 2D physics uses (x, z) of the 3D world." (section 7)
- Audio on the scene PC only; HUD per viewport and shared (section 10). Nothing may block Gate 1 ("one phone steers a box boat on the TV").

Coordinate convention used everywhere here (to be fixed in `shared/map.ts` by the base phase): world X/Z are the 2D sim axes, Y up, water at y = 0; a yaw `theta` means forward = `(cos theta, 0, sin theta)`; a three.js object modelled with its bow along +X faces that way with `rotation.y = -theta`. Column `c` of the ASCII grid is at `x = (c + 0.5 - cols/2) * 8`, row `r` at `z = (r + 0.5 - rows/2) * 8`.

## 3. Algorithms and code

Each snippet names its target path. The two `shared/*` files are the slice of the contract the renderer needs; they must be merged into the real `shared/map.ts` / `shared/state.ts` (owned by the base phase), not copied beside them. The code imports `@shared/*` through the repo's path alias and `three/addons/*` through three's package exports (also mapped by `@types/three`).

Per-frame algorithm (`renderer.ts`):

1. `timer.update()`; `dt = min(0.1, getDelta())`; `renderer.info.reset()`; if `dynamicShadows` then `shadowMap.needsUpdate = true`.
2. Update visuals from the state: `water.update(time)`; boats (`GondolaView.update(boat, alpha, time, dt)`: interpolation, bob/roll/pitch, sink, wake, crew); pools (`count`, matrices, instance colours); `vfx.onEvents`, `vfx.update`, `audio.onEvents`.
3. `setScissorTest(false); clear(colour, depth)`.
4. For each team `i` with `layout.cells[i]`: `r = toGlViewport(inset(cell, border), layout.height)`; `setViewport(r)`, `setScissor(r)`, `setScissorTest(true)`; `rig.setAspect(r.w / r.h)` (updates `fov` and the projection matrix only when the aspect changed); `updateRig` (follow or kill-cam); `cullByDistance(world.cullables, cameraPos, cullDistance)`; `vfx.setViewport(r.h, fov)`; `renderer.render(scene, rig.camera)`.
5. `setScissorTest(false)`; `perf.sample(...)`; step the quality down if advised.

Layout algorithm (`layout.ts`): `gridFor(n)` -> (cols, rows); cell edges at `round(i * size / n)`; cells in row-major order, the first unused index is the spare cell; `minimap` is the spare cell inset by the border or a `0.2 * min(W, H)` square centred on the screen; `scoreboard` is a `0.34 W x 0.16 H` band at the top centre. `placeWidget(cell, corner, w, h, margin, reserved)` positions a HUD widget at a corner and, while it intersects a reserved rect, pushes it along the axis with the smaller push toward the cell centre (two passes).

Camera rig numbers (`camera.ts`): `verticalFovDeg(aspect) = clamp(2 atan(tan(42.5 deg) / aspect), 50, 75)`; `k = 1 - exp(-lambda dt)` with `lambda = 10` (follow) or 4 (kill-cam); wall march in 0.5 m steps from 2.5 m outward along the boat-to-camera segment, stop one step before the first wall cell.

Water shader replacement (`water.ts`), inserted in place of `#include <normal_fragment_maps>`:

```glsl
vec3 nA = texture2D( normalMap, vNormalMapUv * uScaleA + uTime * uFlowA ).xyz * 2.0 - 1.0;
vec3 nB = texture2D( normalMap, vNormalMapUv * uScaleB + uTime * uFlowB ).xyz * 2.0 - 1.0;
vec3 mapN = normalize( vec3( nA.xy + nB.xy, nA.z * nB.z ) );
mapN.xy *= normalScale;
normal = normalize( tbn * mapN );
```

with `uScaleA = 1400 / 9`, `uScaleB = 1400 / 3.3` (tile sizes in metres over the 1400 m plane) and flows of ~0.02 tiles/s in two directions.

Particle motion (`vfx.ts`, vertex shader): `t = uTime - aBirth`; `k = (1 - exp(-drag t)) / drag` (or `t` when drag is 0); `p = p0 + v k + g t^2 / 2`; `gl_PointSize = min(256, size * (1 + 1.5 age) * H / (2 tan(vfov/2)) / depth)`; alpha fades over the last 45 % of life.

Hull loft (`gondola.ts`): station `s in [0, 1]`, `bulge = sin(pi s)`, `width = 1.4 bulge^0.55`, `depth = 0.5 bulge^0.7 + 0.05`, `yTop = 0.42 + 1.1 (1 - bulge)^3`, keel offset `zc = 0.1 bulge`; profile angle `th in [0, pi]`: `z = zc - (width/2) cos th`, `y = yTop - (yTop + depth) sin th`; quads between neighbouring stations and profile points; a two-vertex deck strip under the gunwale; `computeVertexNormals()`.

Block decomposition (`buildings.ts`): scan row-major; at an unused `B`, grow right while `B` and unused (max 6), then grow down while the whole row segment is `B` and unused (max 6); mark used. Windows go on every 8 m edge segment whose outside neighbour cell is not `B`/`T`, two per segment per floor at +-2 m, floors from `1.5 + 1.9 m` up every 3.3 m; a window is skipped automatically where two merged rectangles touch (the neighbour is `B`). Bridges: runs of `=` along X if `B` sits at both X ends, else along Z; arch hole `y = 3.5 sin(pi t)` over the span minus 1.6 m; extrude depth 5 m centred in the 8 m cell.

## 4. Edge cases and how each is handled

| Case | Handling (where) |
|---|---|
| Team count 2..6 and the spare cell | `computeLayout` clamps to 2..6, spare index = first unused cell (3 teams -> cell 3, 5 teams -> cell 5); tested that cells + spare tile the canvas exactly for 1920x1080 and 1366x768. |
| Odd pixel widths, 3 columns in 1920 px | Edges at `round(i*W/3)` = 0, 640, 1280, 1920: no 1 px gaps or overlaps (test). |
| Minimap or scoreboard overlapping a cell's corner widgets | `placeWidget` slides the widget toward the cell centre (test: bottom-right cell's team name moves >= 108 + 14 px right). |
| Resize / fullscreen / projector plugged in late | `resize()` re-reads `canvas.clientWidth/Height`, `setSize(w, h, false)`; the scene recomputes the layout from the same numbers and calls `hud.setLayout`. Layout is otherwise fixed for the round (spec). |
| Non-integer DPR seams (`round` vs `floor`) | `setPixelRatio(1)` (or the quality tier's 0.75, which then applies to the whole canvas uniformly). |
| Shadow map rebuilt per viewport | `autoUpdate = false`; `needsUpdate` is reset by three at the end of the first shadow pass (`WebGLShadowMap.js:376`), so only one pass per frame even in dynamic mode. If no shadow-casting light is in the render (`lights.length === 0`, line 97) the flag stays set: harmless. Re-enabling shadows after a quality step sets `needsUpdate = true` again. |
| Camera inside a building in an 8 m side canal when the gunner looks sideways | `clampCameraToWater` grid march, min 2.5 m; `near = 0.5`. Fallback if it still clips: building materials are `FrontSide`, so from inside a block you see through its back faces instead of a black screen. |
| Kill-cam when the killer is also dead or unknown (mutual ram, mine owner respawned) | `updateRig` falls back to `follow` on the own (sinking) boat; the HUD shows "SUNK" without a team. |
| Kill-cam -> respawn cut | Mode change back to `follow` clears the smoothed position (`settled = false`), so the camera snaps to the respawn dock instead of flying through walls; the wake and crew are reset on `alive` rising edge or a > 20 m jump. |
| Interpolating across a respawn teleport | `GondolaView.update`: a jump > 20 m between `p*` and current uses `alpha = 1`. Angles use `lerpAngle` (shortest arc) from `shared/math.ts`. |
| Campanile beyond the fog | Linear fog reaches 100 % at 140 m; the campanile's material uses `weakenFog` (fog factor x 0.55) so it stays a 45 % silhouette anywhere on the map; it is never distance-culled; `camera.far = 450` covers the 500 m diagonal. |
| Lantern glow seen through walls | The glow sprite keeps `depthTest` on, so a building hides it, consistent with the hiding rule; it has `fog: false` so it reads at range in the open. |
| `gl_PointSize` limits per GPU | Capped at 256 px; the explosion column is a spray burst plus a glow burst rather than one giant point. |
| InstancedMesh pools and frustum culling | Dynamic pools set `frustumCulled = false`; static instanced meshes call `computeBoundingSphere()` once after the matrices are set. |
| `renderer.info` per render call | `autoReset = false`, one `reset()` per frame; the overlay divides calls by the viewport count. |
| HDRI missing, offline, or slow | The procedural `Sky` + PMREM is built synchronously on startup; `applyHdri` is fire-and-forget after frame 2 and catches errors. Nothing blocks Gate 1. |
| AudioContext suspended | Created lazily in `unlock()` from the Start click / Space keydown; `resume()` if suspended; missing sound files are silently skipped. |
| WebGL context lost | `webglcontextlost` logs and three's `render()` becomes a no-op (`_isContextLost` check at the top of `render()`); the scene shows a reload message. |
| Tab hidden / rAF paused | `Timer` clamps `dt` to 0.1 s in `render()`; the sim loop owns its own accumulator. |
| `scene.background` as a `Color` instead of a texture | `WebGLBackground.render` then force-clears inside the current scissor on every viewport: correct, slightly wasteful; with the PMREM texture no clear happens. |
| Transparent sorting with four cameras | three sorts the render list per `render()` call relative to that camera (`sortObjects`), so wakes, sprites and bubbles sort correctly per viewport. |
| 16-bit index overflow when merging | `BufferGeometry.setIndex` picks `Uint32BufferAttribute` when needed (`arrayNeedsUint32`); blocks are far below the limit anyway (4 boxes, 96 vertices). |
| Water plane edge visible | 1400 m plane, fog at 140 m: the edge is 500 m beyond the fog from any camera on the 400 x 300 map. |
| Shutters/windows cost when triangle-bound | 4.6 k windows x (frame + glass + 2 shutters) as boxes is ~220 k triangles per viewport; the "detail" cut hides them, or swap the box geometries for `PlaneGeometry` quads (12 -> 2 triangles each). |
| Upgrade to three r187 | The migration guide says `setViewport()/setScissor()` stop scaling by the pixel ratio when a render target is bound; our calls target the canvas, unaffected, but worth a re-test. |
